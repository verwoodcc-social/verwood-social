# Privacy notice: Cricket Social Kit

This app is used by a cricket club to post its own match fixtures and results to its own
Facebook Page and Instagram account.

**What it uses.** Fixtures, results and scorecards from the club's own Play-Cricket site, which
are already public. For junior teams it shows only a first name and surname initial, or no names,
depending on the club's settings.

**What it stores.** The graphics it makes, in the club's own GitHub repository, and a list of which
matches have been posted. It does not collect, store or share any data about people who view or
interact with the club's posts.

**Facebook and Instagram access.** The app only publishes posts to the club's own Page and
Instagram account, after a club volunteer approves each one (unless the club turns on automatic
posting). It does not read messages, comments, followers or any other account data.

**Deletion.** The club can delete any graphic from its repository, or delete the repository
entirely. To remove the app's access, remove the system user or the app in Meta Business settings.

**Contact.** Contact the club through its Facebook Page.
